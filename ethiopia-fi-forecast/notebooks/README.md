# Exploratory Data Analysis Summary

## Key Insights

1. Account ownership increased from 14% in 2011 to 49% in 2024.

2. Growth slowed significantly between 2021 and 2024 (+3 percentage points), despite rapid mobile money expansion.

3. Mobile money account ownership accelerated after Telebirr (2021) and M-Pesa (2023), but this did not translate into equivalent gains in overall account ownership.

4. Infrastructure-related indicators appear positively associated with digital payment adoption.

5. Several indicators have sparse historical coverage, limiting the complexity of forecasting models and increasing forecast uncertainty.

## Data Quality Limitations

- Limited historical observations.
- Missing values for some indicators.
- Mixed confidence levels across sources.
- Event impacts are partly modeled rather than directly observed.
